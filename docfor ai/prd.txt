1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project Name: ShikshaSign Live (Working Title for ISL Connect)
Target: GRAVITON 2026 (Problem Statement #30)
Core Objective: A browser-based classroom accessibility platform that listens to a teacher's lecture, displays live captions, and presents curriculum-specific Indian Sign Language (ISL) animations/videos on a smart TV or projector.Target Audience: Deaf and hard-of-hearing students, government school teachers, and special educators.

Scope & MVP (Minimum Viable Product):

Curriculum Limit: One complete science lesson (e.g., "Plants").

Vocabulary: 20–30 supported educational phrases/concepts (e.g., "sunlight", "water", "plants need sunlight").

Safety First: No hallucinated signs. If the system does not recognize a supported concept, it falls back to text captions only.

Core Features (Must-Haves):

Teacher Microphone Input: Captures live audio in Hindi/English.

Live Captions: Real-time speech-to-text displayed on screen.

Concept Matching Engine: Maps recognized text to the approved ISL lesson library.

ISL Playback: Plays pre-validated ISL video clips or animations.

Fullscreen Projector Mode: Optimized dual-panel UI for classroom Smart TVs/Projectors.

Safe Fallback: Captions-only mode for unsupported phrases.

Success Metrics (For the Pitch):

Speech Word Error Rate (WER): ≤25% in quiet conditions.

Sign Phrase Precision: ≥90% for supported phrases.

End-to-End Latency: ≤4 seconds.
